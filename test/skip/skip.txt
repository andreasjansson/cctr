===
nested parens in a skip reason are reported in full
===
cctr $CCTR_FIXTURE_DIR/tests/paren_reasons.txt --no-color -v 2>&1 | grep '⊘.*skip reason containing'
---
⊘ tests/paren_reasons: skip reason containing a parenthetical (gets Terra (4.00) instead of Luna)

===
a condition containing parens stays the condition
===
cctr $CCTR_FIXTURE_DIR/tests/paren_reasons.txt --no-color -v 2>&1 | grep '⊘.*command substitution'
---
⊘ tests/paren_reasons: condition containing a command substitution (reason here)
